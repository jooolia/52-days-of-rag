# 52 days of RAG

**7 Nov to 28 Dec 2026.** First the DeepLearning.AI *Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)* course on Coursera, then a small RAG over the Gamarello docs.

## Rules

1. **Weekdays:** 35 minutes, after exercise and the shower.
2. **Saturday:** one long session (60 to 90 min) for the lab. Do it after your weekend writing, not instead of it.
3. **Sunday:** 10 minutes, and that's it.
4. **Evenings are for reading and writing.** No challenge work after dinner.
5. **Bad day?** Do 10 minutes and it counts. Missing a day doesn't break anything. Missing two in a row does.
6. **Log it:** tick the box, then add one short note after it. That's the whole system.

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Sun | Preview this week's module. Choose one idea from last week for your evening notebook. | 10 min |
| Mon to Thu | Videos and readings (2 to 3 videos a day) | 35 min |
| Fri | Graded quiz | 35 min |
| Sat | Programming assignment and ungraded labs | 60 to 90 min |

If a module runs long, let it spill into the next week and cut project days from the end. Finishing the course matters more.
